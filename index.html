Frequently asked questions

How much will I pay for my first session?

If you have insurance, you'll verify your plan when you book and we'll show your exact session cost before you confirm — and guarantee it. That price won't change, even if:

  • Your insurance changes or lapses
  • Your insurer denies the claim
  • Your therapist bills a different code
  • Your deductible turns out to be different than expected

If you don't have insurance, you can pay cash — individual therapist rates are shown on their profile. Applies to your first session; not yet available for Medicare or Medicaid.

How does the Specialty Filter work? Can I find a therapist that focuses on a specific issue?

Select one or more specialties under "Needs" and we'll only show providers who list that focus area.

Why use Gender, Age, and Identities filters?

Many clients feel most comfortable working with a therapist who shares or understands their background. These filters are optional.

What to expect during your first session?

Your first session is about getting to know each other — your therapist will ask about what brought you to therapy and what you're hoping to get out of it. There's no homework due before you start.
